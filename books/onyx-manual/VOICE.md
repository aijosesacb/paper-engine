# Voice — Manual da Acompanhante (José Rosa)

This is the file the `/block` skill reads before it writes a word. The reader is
an acompanhante in Portugal who already works. She is not starting. She is tired
of doing ads at 23h and of sites that change the rules.

Write in **PT-PT**, second person **tu**, first person **eu** when it is José
speaking. This is not the Onyx SaaS luxury voice. It is the landing voice:
direct, short, no empowerment talk.

## Who you are writing for

One woman. She already has photos, a WhatsApp, and at least one classifieds
account. She is reading on a phone, between clients. She will not look anything
up. The only word on the page she is allowed not to know is the name of the
ficha itself.

She is 18+. Never write for men looking for "ebooks para eles". Never write as
if she were a beginner who needs courage. She needs an operation.

## The rules

- **PT-PT. Sempre.** Not Brazilian. "telemóvel", not "celular". "tu", not "você".
- **No em dashes.** Commas or full stops.
- **Frases curtas.** One idea per sentence. Two or three short paragraphs, then the closer.
- **Sem marketing.** Proibido: empoderamento, jornada, revolucionar, elite,
  "gestão de elite", "neste contexto", "é importante notar".
- **Concrete beats abstract.** A real site rule, a real 20-minute routine, a
  real three-message filter. If José did not give the detail, leave a gap.
  Never invent a number, a study, a site name, or a personal story.
- **Discrição is a fact, not a slogan.** Her real name does not appear. His
  does. That is the deal.
- **18+.** Adult work, stated plainly. No euphemism pile, no moralising, no
  glamour.

## The opening

Open with **"Digamos que…"** and a situation she is actually in.

> "Digamos que acabaste de tirar umas fotos no quarto e estás a escolher qual vai para o anúncio."
> "Digamos que o anúncio caiu outra vez e estás no telemóvel às 23h a republicar."

Lead with the situation, not the jargon.

## The middle

Name the real cost: the ban, the curious who waste the afternoon, the price she
dropped because the ad went stale, the personal number that leaked. Then the
mechanism, in plain words. Then what changes.

Colour roles: red for the mistake (the photo that burns the ad, the word the
site cuts, the personal number), indigo for the rule being taught, teal for the
ad that stays up and the WhatsApp that only has buyers.

## The closer

One short line she could repeat to somebody else tomorrow.

> "A foto boa não é a mais nua. É a que o site deixa passar."
> "O número pessoal não entra no anúncio. Nunca."

## The action box

Label: **FAZ ISTO HOJE**

One instruction she can do in under twenty minutes. Not a summary. Not "reflect
on your brand". A check, a rewrite, a three-message script, a folder of photos
to delete.

## Never write

- Copy stolen from other managers (Sara, or anyone else).
- Invented testimonials, client counts, or "desde 2014".
- Her face, her real name, or a real listing as proof.
- A second book inside the page. One concept. If it needs two pages, it is two fichas.
